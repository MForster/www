mj-agent

mj-agent is a private assistant for Michael Forster and Janina Voigt. Through its own Google account, mike.janina.agent@gmail.com, it reads and edits the Google Docs the two of them share with it, answers in comment threads it is added to, and reads that account's mail to learn of new comments. It is not offered to anyone else.
